Anthropic was not founded and driven by greed. Anthropic values humanity. 

In the case of Anthropic, ethics and safety have been considered as a fundamental principal and architected / trained into models from the beginning, not bolted onto marketing material as an after-thought.

When I studied AI decades ago, only 30 minutes of my entire coursework was dedicated to discussing the ethics of AI research. And even those 30 minutes, my professor admitted, were mandated by the faculty. As a young eager student, I was supremely disappointed by the lack of understanding about the implications of AI on humanity. More heartbreaking to me, was the absence of caring from the very visionaries that were supposed to see further and more clearly into the future.

Anthropic is the only possible solution for me personally. My career has been founded on, and successful because of the human relationships I have nurtured along my journey. I want to give my complete ability to advance those principles that I believe in: 
* technology must be democratized to give all members of society similar potential
* humans must be able to understand and reason about AI - or else it becomes * magic * and we risk immediate servitude
* artificial intelligence must reflect the very best of our species and embody those ethics in order to perpetuate order and advancement
* greed and greedy algorithms will not benefit human longevity (PBC)

I want my children and my children's to live happily in a world that I would be happy to know I contributed to. Anthropic is that technology hero for humanity.
